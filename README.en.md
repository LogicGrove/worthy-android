# Worthy

[Español](README.md) | **English**

**Save for the things you want.**

Worthy turns the things you want to buy into savings goals. Add a product and its price, record the money you set aside, and see how much you have saved, how much is left, and how you are progressing.

You can keep several goals and move between them like a deck of cards. Goals and contributions are stored on your device. Worthy records your savings; it does not transfer money or connect to your bank.

## Download and install

**[Download Worthy 1.0 for Android](https://github.com/LogicGrove/worthy-android/releases/tag/v1.0)** · Android 7.0 or later.

1. Open the release and download `Worthy-1.0.apk` on your phone.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installation from the app you used to download it.
3. Install Worthy and create your first goal. This version's interface is in English.

If you previously used a development build and Android rejects the update, export a backup from Worthy before uninstalling: uninstalling may erase your data. See [updates and signing](docs/FIRMA_ANDROID.en.md).

## How to use it

1. **Choose something you want:** create a goal and enter its price.
2. **Add contributions:** record the money you are setting aside.
3. **Check your progress:** see how much you have saved and what remains.
4. **Reach your goal:** keep going until you get there.

## Features

- Multiple goals with cards and visual progress.
- Light and dark themes, personalization, and supported dynamic colors.
- Optional haptic feedback and a high refresh rate setting.
- Backup and restore. Restoring a backup replaces your current goals and contributions.
